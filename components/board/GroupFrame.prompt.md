Groups a region of a board — a platform boundary, a squad's surface area, the legend.

```jsx
<GroupFrame title="Authenticated shell">
  {/* nodes */}
</GroupFrame>
```

Solid border = a real boundary that exists today; `dashed` = proposed, out of scope, or future phase. The title chip paints `--canvas-bg` behind itself, so a frame must sit directly on the board, not on top of another fill.
